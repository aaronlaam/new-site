CONNECTSMART

AI Intake & Workflow Automation

Simplifying access. Supporting staff. Improving outcomes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT CONNECTSMART DOES

ConnectSmart is a modern, automated intake assistant designed for human services, behavioral health, and community support programs. It responds instantly, guides callers with natural language, and routes them to the correct department while collecting essential intake information.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHY IT MATTERS

• Calls currently go to voicemail
• Staff are overwhelmed with callbacks
• No real-time crisis or urgency screening
• Manual FormDr workflows slow the process
• Missed opportunities for early intervention
• Language barriers delay access to care

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

DEPARTMENT WORKFLOWS SUPPORTED

MENTAL HEALTH
• Crisis screening
• Adult, child, and family therapy pathways
• Automatic FormDr packet delivery

PSS (DOMESTIC VIOLENCE & SEXUAL ABUSE)
• Trauma-informed language
• Silent mode option for unsafe callers
• Safety and risk-level screening

BAMH (BILINGUAL ADULT MENTAL HEALTH)
• Automatic English/Spanish recognition
• Spanish-first workflow
• Culturally appropriate form sets

SENIOR SERVICES
• Mobility and safety questions
• Outlook appointment scheduling
• Detailed intake notes

CASE MANAGEMENT
• Benefits and insurance assistance
• Family support needs
• Resource Center pathways

FOOD PANTRY
• Household size and eligibility screening
• Emergency detection ("no food tonight")
• Immediate alerts for urgent cases

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

HOW IT WORKS (AT A GLANCE)

1. Caller speaks naturally
   "I need therapy."
   "I'm calling about food."
   "I need domestic violence support."

2. ConnectSmart identifies intent
   The AI understands the caller and routes them to the appropriate departmental agent.

3. Agent collects key information
   Name, phone number, need, language, urgency indicators, and any necessary screening details.

4. FormDr packet sent instantly
   Tailored to Mental Health, PSS, BAMH, Case Management, Senior Services, or the Food Pantry.

5. Intake ticket created for the team
   Clear, structured, actionable information ready before the callback.
